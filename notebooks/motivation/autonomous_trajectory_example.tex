\begin{example}
Consider the autonomous system
\(y_{k+2}=\tfrac12(y_k+y_{k+1}^2)\), whose state is
\(x_k=(y_k,y_{k+1})\).
For \(L=3\), each complete trajectory is
\[
    w(a,b)=\operatorname{col}\!\left(a,b,\tfrac12(a+b^2)\right)
    \in\mathbb{R}^3,
\]
where \((a,b)=(y_0,y_1)\in[-1,1]^2\).
These trajectories form the two-dimensional curved surface
\(y_2=\tfrac12(y_0+y_1^2)\).
Each point is a full length-three output window, of the same form as a
column of the depth-three output Hankel matrix; hence the surface is
visualized directly in trajectory space without dimensionality reduction.
\end{example}
